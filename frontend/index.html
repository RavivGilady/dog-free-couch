    Cameras

    A camera watches the couch where it stands — detection runs there, and only events, clips and (while you are watching) frames come here. That can be the agent on the computer the camera is plugged into, or this browser itself.

    Use this device as a camera

    Turns the computer or phone you are reading this on into a camera station: this tab watches through its camera, detects the dog itself, and sounds the alarm out of these speakers. Nothing to install.

    Keep this tab open and the screen awake, and leave the device plugged in — detection and an open camera are hungry. The first start downloads the detection model (a few MB).
