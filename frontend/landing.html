Questions

What do I need?

A camera that can see the couch: a laptop, a phone, a USB webcam or a Raspberry Pi camera, plus speakers near the couch for the alarm. A Telegram account if you want alerts on your phone.

Do I have to install anything?

No, if you use a browser as the camera: open the dashboard on the device and press start. For a computer or Raspberry Pi that runs all the time, there is a one-line installer that also keeps it running in the background.

Will it recognise my dog, or just any dog?

It detects dogs in general, not a particular one, and it does not tell dogs apart. It also counts people in the frame, so you can see who is on the couch.

Who can see my video?

Only your account. Your clips are stored on the server and are reachable only when you are signed in. Live view and alerts are not shared with anyone else.

Does it work in the dark?

It depends on your camera. Detection needs a reasonably clear picture, so a lamp or a camera with night vision helps.

What if something does not work?

The help centre covers the common problems, including a camera that shows a grey or frozen picture. You can also email us.
